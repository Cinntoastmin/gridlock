Gridlock
------------------------

a turn based card automation game. where players use a deck of cards create a factory
to meet a production target.

the game will start with the 3x3 factory grid blank. at the start of the players first turn they will draw 
4 cards as a starting hand. the player may select any cards from their initial hand 
to mulligan. after these are selected they are set aside, new cards are drawn in their 
place, and then the selected mulligan cards are shuffled back into the deck.

at the start of the game the players deck will consist of 10 cards:
    4 production buildings which increment the production of the factory by one when placed
    3 enhancement buildings which provide no production bonus by themselves, 
        but instead increase the production of adjacent production buildings
    2 augments which can be played on top of an exsisting building to increase their level. 
        which enhances their default behavior. 
        i.e. production builds will increase their base production
        and enhancement buildings will increase their production multiplier
    1 "Overclock" which immediately generates the current factories production value and adds it to the total

a turn ends after the player plays two cards (this value can be upgraded between rounds)

at the end of the turn the current production value of the factory is added to the total round production
then if the total round production exceeds the quota the round ends. 

if after the final turn the total round production does not meet or exceed the quota the game ends

after each time the player meets quota a new quota is set at the current quota times a difficulty modifier
    1.5 for easy
    2.0 for medium
    and 2.5 for hard (subject to change given playtest results)
the turn counter is reset to zero and a number of additional turns is given equal to the floor of the difficulty modifier inverse to the current player's selection 
    ie if the player has selected easy they would be given floor(hard_difficulty_modifier) turns

before the player takes their first turn they are give the ability to spend their current production to modify their deck of cards
they may:
    upgrade cards:
        which applies a permanent augment to a building card
        add cards to their deck (details to come)
        remove cards from their deck (minimum deck size 0)
        (more options may come but start here)

then after the player modifies their deck the unspent production is added to their 
next round's total production and the next round begins:
    board is empty
    player draws up to four cards
    player may mulligan any number of cards
    player plays cards to limit
    turn ends
        factory production gets added to it's total round production
        if the total prodcution exceeds the quota trigger the upgrade phase
